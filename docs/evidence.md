# Evidence

Every statistic used in the app, the deck and the video comes from this list. Change a number
here first, then wherever it is used. Figures were checked on 13 October 2026.

Figures from different universities come from different years and survey methods. They show
the same pressure on many campuses. They are not a ranking and should not be compared with
each other.

## The headline: finishing a degree

| Statement | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| About 6 in 10 students who start a degree or diploma graduate within ten years | 61.2% of the 2011 intake, all undergraduate qualifications, contact and distance | DHET cohort report, Feb 2022 |
| 1 in 5 leave after first year | 21.3% of the 2011 intake | DHET cohort report, Feb 2022 |
| 1 in 3 have dropped out by year ten | 32.4% of the 2011 intake | DHET cohort report, Feb 2022 |
| 7 in 10 contact students graduate within six years | 69.9% of the 2015 intake, contact tuition only | DHET cohort report, Feb 2022 |
| Fewer than half of UJ undergraduates finish in minimum time | 44.6% in 2019 | Daily Maverick, Nov 2022, citing UJ |

The report gives reasons for dropping out (under-preparedness, money, wrong course choice,
living conditions) but does not rank them. Do not say "most students leave because of money".

## Do not use

| Claim | Why |
|---|---|
| "Up to 60% of first-years leave after their first term" | One company's estimate. The official figure is 21% after first year |
| "4 in 10 have not finished six years later" | The 4 in 10 figure is after ten years, not six |
| "620,000 NSFAS first-years in 2023, 130,000 still funded a year later" | First-year only, and the official report finds NSFAS-funded students graduate at a higher rate than the national average |
